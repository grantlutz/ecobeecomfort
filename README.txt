Control your ecobee thermostat from Homey — including the comfort profiles the ecobee app calls Home, Away, and Sleep. Switch profiles with a tap or from a Flow, and the thermostat applies that profile's own temperature and fan settings, so an "everyone has left home" Flow can drop the house to Away on its own. Alongside the profiles you get mode, temperature, fan, humidity, and hold status, plus the temperature and motion readings from your ecobee room sensors.

Signing in uses your normal ecobee account, so no separate developer key is required, and two-step verification is supported. This is an unofficial, community-built app and is not affiliated with ecobee.
